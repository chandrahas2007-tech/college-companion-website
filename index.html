About College Companion

College Companion is an Android application designed to help college students organize their everyday academic and personal activities in one place.

From managing daily routines and class schedules to tracking expenses and maintaining important contacts, College Companion aims to make college life more organized and convenient.

Explore Our Features

📆 Week Plan

🏫 Class Timetable

Keep your class timetable organized and easily check your scheduled classes.

💰 Expense Tracker

Record your expenses and keep track of your spending during college life.

🍽️ Mess Menu

👥 Connections

🎓 Academic Calendar

Keep track of important academic dates, events and semester activities.

Why Choose College Companion?

College life involves managing classes, assignments, personal activities and expenses. College Companion brings these organizational tools together in one application.

Its goal is to help students save time, stay organized and manage their everyday college responsibilities more easily.
